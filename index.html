Team

Thomas Ascough

Thomas Ascough is a composer and sound designer specializing in music for film, TV, video games, and animation. His work has appeared in productions worldwide, including placements on Gordon Ramsay and Motorway Cops: Catching Britain. He holds a B.S. in Music Production and an MFA in Scoring and Composition, and continues writing for the Emmy award-winning library Crimesonics while teaching music and recording arts at Fusion Academy.

Visit Thomas's site

Julian Ranieri

Julian Ranieri is a Musican, producer and audio engineer with a background in studio production and software development. After years producing independent artists and working as an audio technician at the Academy of Art University, he expanded into modern music tools while keeping a strong analog production mindset. His approach blends classic outboard workflow with efficient digital systems for practical, creative results.
